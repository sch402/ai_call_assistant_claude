WHAT THE APP DOES
Call Assistant answers calls the user misses. The user turns on conditional call forwarding with their Australian carrier (standard GSM codes such as **004*<number>#). Missed calls are forwarded to our AI assistant (via Twilio and Vapi), which tells the caller it is an AI assistant, asks who is calling and why, and then posts a summary to the app. Calls are not recorded; only the transcript and summary are stored.

Answering a real call requires an Australian mobile SIM with call forwarding, so it cannot be tested outside Australia. The sample calls in the demo account show the result. The 代接设置 (Forwarding) tab only copies the carrier code; iOS does not allow apps to dial forwarding codes, so the user pastes it into the Phone keypad.
