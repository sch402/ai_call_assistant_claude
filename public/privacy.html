隐私政策

来电助手 · Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd · 更新于 2026 年 10 月

我们收集什么

  • 你的手机号：用于短信验证码登录，以及确认被转移过来的来电属于你。
  • AI 代接的来电：来电号码、时间、运营商带来的转移来源、通话文字转写，以及 AI 生成的摘要和分类（例如是否疑似诈骗）。
  • 订阅信息：苹果提供的交易编号、订阅产品和到期时间。付款由苹果处理，我们拿不到你的银行卡等支付信息。

我们不保存通话录音，也不读取你的通讯录、短信或位置。

怎么使用

只用于提供代接服务：接听转过来的电话、生成摘要给你看、判断订阅是否有效。我们不出售数据，不用于广告。

AI 接听时会告诉来电方这是 AI 助手，并且不会透露你的姓名以外的任何个人信息。

谁会处理这些数据

  • Supabase（数据库，澳洲悉尼）
  • Twilio（接收转移来电、发送登录验证码）
  • Vapi 与 OpenAI（实时语音识别与对话，服务器在美国）
  • Vercel（服务托管）
  • Apple（订阅付款）

保存多久、如何删除

通话记录会一直保存，直到你删除账户。你可以在 App 的「我的 › 删除账户」里随时删除，你的手机号、通话记录和订阅记录会被立即永久删除。删除账户不会取消苹果订阅，请在 iPhone 的「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」里取消。如需导出数据，请联系 [联系邮箱]。

Privacy Policy

Call Assistant · Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd · Updated October 2026

What we collect

  • Your phone number, to sign you in with an SMS code and to match forwarded calls to your account.
  • Calls answered by the AI: caller number, time, the forwarding number supplied by your carrier, a text transcript, and an AI-generated summary and category (for example, suspected scam).
  • Subscription details from Apple: transaction ID, product and expiry date. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

We do not store call recordings, and we do not access your contacts, messages or location.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: answering forwarded calls, showing you summaries, and checking your subscription. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. The AI tells callers it is an AI assistant.

Service providers

Supabase (database, Sydney), Twilio (call forwarding and SMS codes), Vapi and OpenAI (real-time speech and conversation, United States), Vercel (hosting) and Apple (payments).

Retention and deletion

Call records are kept until you delete your account. You can do this at any time in the app under Me › Delete Account; your phone number, call records and subscription records are permanently deleted straight away. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel it in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To export your data, contact [联系邮箱].
