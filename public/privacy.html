隐私政策

来电助手 · Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

English用户协议

简要说明
  • 我们只收集提供代接服务所需的信息：你的手机号、助手设置、AI 代接的来电记录和订阅状态。
  • 通话不录音，只保存文字转写和 AI 摘要。
  • 不出售你的数据，不用于广告，不读取你的通讯录、短信或位置。
  • 你可以随时在 App 里删除账户，所有数据会被立即删除。

1. 我们是谁

「来电助手」（以下称「本服务」）由 Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd（以下称「我们」）运营。我们遵守澳大利亚《1988 年隐私法》（Privacy Act 1988）及其中的澳大利亚隐私原则（APPs）。本政策说明我们收集哪些个人信息、为什么收集、如何使用和保护，以及你享有的权利。

2. 我们收集的信息

类别具体内容用途
账户信息你的手机号（通过短信验证码确认）登录；确认被转移过来的来电属于你
助手设置你填写的称呼，以及选择的声音（男声 / 女声、音色）AI 接听时使用你的称呼和你选的声音

黑名单你选择拉黑的号码直接拒接这些号码

我们不会收集：通话录音、你的通讯录、短信、位置、照片，以及银行卡等支付信息（付款完全由 Apple 处理）。

3. 关于来电方的信息

AI 代接时会处理来电方的电话号码和他们在通话中说的话。为此：

  • AI 在通话中会告诉来电方，接听的是 AI 助手。
  • 这些信息只用于把来电内容转达给你，保存在你的账户下，随你的账户一起删除。
  • 来电方如需查询或删除与自己有关的记录，可以通过本政策末尾的方式联系我们。

4. 我们如何使用信息

  • 提供代接服务：接听转过来的电话、生成摘要、识别诈骗和推销电话。
  • 管理你的账户和订阅。
  • 保障服务安全，防止滥用和欺诈。
  • 排查故障、改进服务质量（使用汇总或去标识化的数据）。
  • 履行法律义务。

我们不出售或出租你的个人信息，不用于广告或营销画像，也不会用你的通话内容训练我们自己的 AI 模型。

5. AI 处理

来电的实时语音识别、对话和摘要由第三方 AI 服务完成（见第 6 节）。通话音频只在通话过程中被实时处理，我们已关闭录音功能，不保存音频。AI 生成的摘要和分类可能不准确，请以完整转写为准。按 OpenAI 的 API 数据政策，通过 API 提交的数据默认不会被用于训练其模型。

6. 我们与谁共享信息

我们只把信息提供给帮助我们运营本服务的服务商，并要求他们按合同保护数据、只用于为我们提供服务：

服务商用途数据所在地

Vercel服务器托管澳大利亚（悉尼），部分服务可能在美国

除此以外，只有在法律要求（例如法院命令）、为保护用户或公众的安全、或公司合并收购时，我们才会披露个人信息，并在法律允许时通知你。

7. 跨境传输

如第 6 节所列，部分信息会被传输到澳大利亚境外（主要是美国）处理。使用本服务即表示你知悉并同意这种传输。我们会采取合理措施，确保境外服务商按与澳大利亚隐私原则相当的标准保护你的信息。

8. 保存期限

  • 账户信息、助手设置、来电记录和黑名单：保存到你删除账户为止。
  • 订阅记录：随账户删除；法律要求保留的交易记录除外。
  • 服务器运行日志：只短期保留，用于排查故障，之后自动删除。

9. 安全

数据在传输中全程加密（HTTPS），数据库访问受严格权限控制，只有服务器能读写。登录凭证只保存在你手机的钥匙串中。没有任何系统能保证绝对安全；如发生可能对你造成严重伤害的数据泄露，我们会按《隐私法》的「应通报数据泄露」（Notifiable Data Breaches）规定通知你和澳大利亚信息专员办公室（OAIC）。

10. 你的权利

  • 查询和更正：可以在 App 里查看和修改你的称呼和声音设置；其他信息可联系我们查询或更正。
  • 删除：在 App 的「我的 › 删除账户」里随时删除，你的手机号、来电记录和订阅记录会被立即永久删除。删除账户不会取消 Apple 订阅，请在 iPhone 的「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」里取消。
  • 导出：如需导出你的来电记录，请联系我们。
  • 投诉：如对我们处理个人信息的方式不满，请先联系我们，我们会在 30 天内回复。如仍不满意，可以向澳大利亚信息专员办公室（OAIC，www.oaic.gov.au）投诉。

11. 未成年人

本服务面向 18 岁及以上的用户，不针对未成年人。如果你认为有未成年人向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们删除。

12. 政策更新

我们可能会更新本政策。重大变更会在 App 内或通过其他合理方式提前通知你。更新后继续使用本服务，即表示你同意更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy

Call Assistant · Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd · Effective 22 October 2026

In short
  • We collect only what we need to answer your calls: your phone number, assistant settings, records of calls the AI answered, and your subscription status.
  • Calls are not recorded. We keep a text transcript and an AI summary.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and we do not access your contacts, messages or location.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time, and all your data is deleted straight away.

1. Who we are

Call Assistant (the "Service") is operated by Everyone Delivery Pty Ltd ("we", "us"). We comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: your mobile number, verified by SMS code, used to sign you in and to match forwarded calls to your account.
  • Assistant settings: the name you give and your voice choice (male/female, style), used when the AI answers.
  • Call records: caller number, time, the forwarding number supplied by your carrier, duration, a text transcript, and an AI-generated summary and category (for example, suspected scam).
  • Blocked numbers you choose to block.
  • Subscription: Apple transaction ID, product, expiry date and auto-renew status.
  • Technical: app version, request times, IP addresses and similar server logs, for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect call audio, your contacts, messages, location or photos, or card details (payments are handled entirely by Apple).

3. Information about callers

When the AI answers, it processes the caller's number and what they say. The AI tells callers they are speaking to an AI assistant. This information is used only to pass the message on to you, is stored under your account and is deleted with it. Callers may contact us to ask about or delete records relating to them.

4. How we use information

To provide the Service (answering forwarded calls, summarising them, flagging scams and sales calls), manage your account and subscription, keep the Service secure and prevent abuse, troubleshoot and improve the Service using aggregated or de-identified data, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell or rent your personal information, use it for advertising, or use your calls to train our own AI models.

5. AI processing

Real-time speech recognition, conversation and summarisation are performed by third-party AI services (see section 6). Call audio is processed in real time only; recording is turned off and we do not store audio. AI summaries and categories may be inaccurate, so rely on the full transcript. Under OpenAI's API data policy, data submitted through the API is not used to train its models by default.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts requiring them to protect it and use it only to provide services to us: Supabase (database and sign-in, Sydney, Australia), Vercel (hosting, Sydney, with some services possibly in the United States), Twilio (call forwarding and SMS codes, United States and elsewhere), Vapi (AI voice platform, United States), OpenAI (speech, conversation and summaries, United States) and Apple (payments). Otherwise we disclose personal information only where required by law, to protect the safety of users or the public, or in a business transfer, and will notify you where the law allows.

7. Overseas disclosure

As listed above, some information is processed outside Australia, mainly in the United States. By using the Service you acknowledge and consent to this. We take reasonable steps to ensure overseas providers protect your information to a standard comparable to the APPs.

8. Retention

Account information, assistant settings, call records and blocked numbers are kept until you delete your account. Subscription records are deleted with your account, except transaction records we must keep by law. Server logs are kept only briefly for troubleshooting and then deleted automatically.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and database access is restricted to our servers. Sign-in credentials are stored only in your phone's Keychain. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

10. Your rights

  • Access and correction: view and change your name and voice settings in the app; contact us about other information.
  • Deletion: delete your account at any time under Me › Delete Account; your phone number, call records and subscription records are permanently deleted straight away. This does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel it in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • Export: contact us to export your call records.
  • Complaints: contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au.

11. Children

The Service is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will give reasonable notice of significant changes in the app or by other reasonable means. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the updated policy.
