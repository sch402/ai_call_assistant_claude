开始使用

  1. 用你的澳洲手机号和短信验证码登录，设置 AI 助手对你的称呼和声音。
  2. 订阅「来电助手 Pro」。
  3. 在「代接设置」里点「开启代接」，代码会自动复制。打开「电话」App › 拨号键盘，长按号码栏粘贴并拨出，看到运营商「设置成功」的提示后回到 App 确认。
  4. 用另一部手机打你的手机，不接，过一会儿在「来电」列表里就能看到这通电话的摘要。

常见问题

需要换号码或者装第二张卡吗？

不需要。来电助手用的是运营商自带的「条件呼叫转移」：你没接、拒接或没信号时，电话才会转给 AI。你的号码和平时用法都不变。

支持哪些运营商？

Telstra、Optus、Vodafone 等主流运营商都支持标准的呼叫转移代码。部分虚拟运营商或预付费套餐可能不支持，或对转移另外收费，可以先咨询运营商。

开启后电话没有转给 AI？

  • 到 iPhone「设置 › App › 电话」关闭「实时语音留言」，并把「筛选陌生来电」设为「不筛选」。这两项开着时，iPhone 会自己先接听。
  • 在「代接设置 › 分项设置与查询」里查询当前的转移设置，确认转到的是 App 里显示的代接号码。
  • 确认订阅有效：订阅过期后，转过来的电话不会被接听。

为什么不能在 App 里直接拨打转移代码？

为了防诈骗，iOS 不允许 App 拨打设置呼叫转移的代码，所以需要你复制后到拨号键盘粘贴拨出。查询和取消代码可以直接拨打。

通话会被录音吗？

不会。我们关闭了录音，只保存文字转写和 AI 摘要。

来电的人知道接电话的是 AI 吗？

知道。AI 会如实说明自己是 AI 助手，不会假装是你本人，不会透露你的姓氏、住址或账户信息，也不会替你答应任何事情。

出国或者不想用了怎么办？

在 App 里点「关闭代接」，或直接拨 ##004# 取消所有条件转移，来电恢复正常。出国前建议先关闭，避免转移产生漫游费用。

怎么取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理：在 iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」中随时取消，可以用到当前周期结束。换了新手机，在订阅页点「恢复购买」即可。

怎么删除账户和数据？

在 App 里「我的 › 删除账户」，账户和所有来电记录会被立即删除。删除账户不会自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不会取消运营商的呼叫转移，请分别取消。

Getting started

  1. Sign in with your Australian mobile number and an SMS code, then choose the name and voice your AI assistant uses.
  2. Subscribe to Call Assistant Pro.
  3. In the Forwarding tab, tap the button to turn answering on; the code is copied for you. Open the Phone app › Keypad, paste the code and dial it. When your carrier confirms, come back and confirm in the app.
  4. Call your mobile from another phone and don't answer. The summary appears in the Calls list shortly after.

FAQ

Do I need a new number or a second SIM?

No. Call Assistant uses your carrier's conditional call forwarding: only calls you miss, decline or can't take go to the AI. Your number stays the same.

Which carriers are supported?

Telstra, Optus, Vodafone and other carriers that support the standard GSM forwarding codes. Some MVNOs and prepaid plans may not support forwarding or may charge for it; check with your carrier.

Calls aren't reaching the AI

  • In iPhone Settings › Apps › Phone, turn off Live Voicemail and set Screen Unknown Callers to Never. When these are on, your iPhone answers first.
  • Use the query codes in the Forwarding tab to check the calls are forwarded to the number shown in the app.
  • Make sure your subscription is active. Forwarded calls are not answered after it expires.

Why can't the app dial the forwarding code for me?

To prevent fraud, iOS doesn't let apps dial codes that set up call forwarding, so you paste it into the keypad yourself. Query and cancel codes can be dialled from the app.

Are calls recorded?

No. Recording is turned off; only the transcript and AI summary are stored.

Do callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The AI says it is an AI assistant, never pretends to be you, never shares your surname, address or account details, and never makes promises on your behalf.

Travelling, or want to stop?

Tap the button to turn answering off in the app, or dial ##004# to cancel all conditional forwarding. Turn it off before travelling overseas to avoid roaming charges.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the current period. On a new phone, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to 我的 (Me) › 删除账户 (Delete Account). Your account and all call records are deleted immediately. This doesn't cancel your Apple subscription or your carrier's call forwarding, so cancel those separately.
